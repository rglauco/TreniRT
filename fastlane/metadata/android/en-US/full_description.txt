TreniRT shows schedules, departures, arrivals and delays for Italian trains in real time, using the same public data from Trenitalia's ViaggiaTreno infrastructure — the same data available on the official website and app.

Main features:

* Departures and arrivals by station, at any time
* Search by train number, with all stops and the real-time delay
* Filter by destination station, with automatic search for connections/train changes when there is no direct train
* History of recent searches by station and by train number
* Light and dark theme
* Automatic (infinite scroll) and manual data refresh
* No ads, no trackers, no personal data collection

TreniRT is an independent, hobby project, not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Trenitalia, RFI, or Gruppo FS.
